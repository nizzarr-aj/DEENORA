DEENORA Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

DEENORA (دينورا) is an Islamic companion application providing prayer times, Quran reading and audio, Azkar, Qibla, Tasbih, Islamic calendar, Names of Allah, duas, stories and quizzes.

For privacy questions or requests, contact the privacy contact shown on the DEENORA Google Play listing. Before publishing this policy, the developer should replace that mechanism with a direct privacy email or contact form.

1. Information We Access

  • Location: DEENORA may request approximate or precise device location when you choose to use location-based prayer times or location-related features. Location is used to calculate relevant prayer times and improve location selection.
  • Notifications: If you enable prayer notifications, the app uses Android notification functionality to schedule and display prayer reminders.
  • Device sensors: The Qibla feature may use the device's compass/magnetic sensors to determine direction. Sensor readings are used locally for the Qibla feature.
  • App settings: Preferences such as language, selected city, prayer calculation method, display settings, Quran font size and similar settings may be stored locally on your device.
  • Bookmarks and local content: Quran bookmarks and similar personal app preferences may be stored locally on your device.

2. Quran Audio and Internet Services

When you choose to play Quran audio, DEENORA connects to an external audio service to retrieve the requested recitation. The app does not intentionally collect or sell your personal information through the audio feature. External service providers may process technical connection information as part of normal Internet communication. Their own policies govern their services.

The app also uses Internet access for features that require online resources.

3. How Information Is Used

Information and device capabilities accessed by DEENORA are used only to provide or support app functionality, including:

  • calculating prayer times for your selected location;
  • providing Qibla direction;
  • sending prayer reminders when you enable notifications;
  • saving your app preferences and Quran bookmarks locally;
  • providing Quran audio when you request playback;
  • maintaining and improving the reliability and functionality of the app.

4. Sharing and Sale of Data

DEENORA does not sell your personal or sensitive data. The app does not intentionally share your location, bookmarks or personal preferences with advertisers or other parties for sale. When an external service is required to provide a feature, such as retrieving Quran audio, a network request may be made to that service. Third-party services have their own privacy practices.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Most DEENORA preferences and bookmarks are stored locally on your device. You can remove locally stored app data by using the Android system's app-data controls or by uninstalling the application, subject to Android and device behavior.

DEENORA does not currently require you to create an account to use its core features, so there is no DEENORA account database to delete for a standard local-only user.

6. Children's Privacy

DEENORA is not designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for advertising or profiling purposes.

7. Security

DEENORA uses Android platform security mechanisms and HTTPS for supported network communications. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when DEENORA adds or changes features, data practices or third-party services. The updated version will be published at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, requests or concerns, use the privacy contact mechanism provided on the official DEENORA Google Play listing.

Important: before submitting this policy to Google Play, the developer should replace the contact mechanism above with a real privacy email address or working contact form.

10. Current Scope

This policy describes the DEENORA version currently being prepared for Google Play. If advertising, subscriptions, analytics, authentication, cloud storage or other third-party SDKs are added, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration must be updated before release.
