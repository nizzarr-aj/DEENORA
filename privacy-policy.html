DEENORA Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Welcome to DEENORA, an Islamic application designed to provide prayer times, Quran reading and audio, Azkar, Tasbih, Qibla direction, Islamic calendar, Islamic names, educational content and other useful features.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and how third-party services may process information when you use DEENORA.

1. Information We May Collect

DEENORA may process limited information necessary to provide certain application features.

  • Location information: approximate or precise device location may be used when you enable location access, mainly to calculate local prayer times and Qibla direction.
  • Application preferences: settings such as language, theme, Quran preferences, bookmarks and similar choices may be stored locally on your device.
  • Device and technical information: third-party advertising and analytics services may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, operating system information and application interactions.

DEENORA does not require you to provide your name, phone number, address or payment card
